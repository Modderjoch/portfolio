summary: |
  For the GMTK Game Jam 2026 I finally had some time to take part. In the years before I had always been busy, but this jam fell right in the middle of my holiday.
  The theme was 'Counting Down", based on that theme I decided to go with a game about counting down sheep to help you fall asleep. The game contains some roguelike mechanics like the shop in between rounds, you score based on the fur and body value of the played animal and there is a fun breeding mechanic that can lead to all sorts of combinations!
  I created the pixel art myself, which was a first, it felt good to be learning something new and I received positive comments on the assets I made, which is always nice.
